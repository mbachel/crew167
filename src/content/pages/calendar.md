## intro

Crew 167 holds regular meetings on the 1st and 3rd Tuesday of every month. On occasion, we do a special meeting when a 5th Tuesday falls upon the calendar. Along with meetings, the Crew strives to hold one activity a month. Activities can be weekend long camping trips, like backpacking, or a day event like volunteering for the Novant Marathon.
