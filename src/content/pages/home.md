## venturing

Venturing is Scouting America's premier program for young men and women who are 14 and up, or 13 and finished with eighth grade, and under 21. Venturing is built around Adventure, Leadership, Personal growth, and Service, known as the ALPS model.

Through Venturing, you will make friendships that last, while doing the things that matter to you. Along the way, you pick up some things that really matter later in life: planning events, running meetings, keeping budgets, and adventuring safely.

## about

Crew 167 was chartered in 2015 and, like many other Crews, has had its ups and downs. However, each time we rebuild, we come back stronger. For us, it's not about the quantity, it's about the quality. 

Each iteration of Crew 167 comes with its own uniqueness. As members join, the culture changes, but the ideals stay the same. Members may move on to different things, but the impact of Venturing stays with them, and the impact they left on the Crew remains as well.

## crew-car

On a Grayson Highlands backpacking trip in 2021, someone spotted a small green matchbox car lying in the dirt on the trail. It came home with us, and it never left.

A Crew President turned it into a tradition. At each meeting the Crew Car is awarded to the member who best showed the ideals of the Scout Oath and Law. That person keeps the car until the next meeting, takes it everywhere they go, and photographs it along the way. The pictures end up on our Instagram, showcasing the places the Crew Car has been.

It has been handed down at every meeting since. Losing it is not an option anybody wants to test.

## adventures

Twice a year we hold planning meetings where members bring their best and brightest ideas on adventures we can take part in. Everyone gets a say in what we do and when we do it. Every event is planned by two youth and an adult, giving everyone equal opportunity.

We've gone backpacking, hiking, whitewater rafting, horseback riding, city exploring, caving, climbing, and many others. Some traditions last through different iterations of Crew culture, like Thanksgiving in the Woods, Winterfest, and Rendezvous.

## join

Come to any of our meetings for free as a prospective Venturer. Meet the Crew leadership and get to know a lot more about us and what we do. Visitors are welcome at any regular meeting without registration, and you're welcome to bring a parent along.

If you'd rather ask questions before you walk in the door, email us and a real person will write back.
